Home / My progress

My progress

All your marks in one place: lessons, project steps, exercises and the final test. Progress is kept only in this browser, but you can move it to another device with a file.

Summary

Tick “Step done” and solve exercises: this is where you will see how much is done.

The summary needs JavaScript.

Move to another device

Download the file here and upload it on this same page on your phone or another computer.

The file contains only progress marks, no personal data. Loading it replaces your current progress.

Certificate

The certificate opens when you score at least 12 out of 15 in the final test.

In the print window, choose a printer or “Save as PDF”. The name is not sent anywhere. This is a learning certificate for self-assessment, not an official document.
